GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Do you offer online consultations?+

Yes. Consultations are available online as well as in person in Pune, Maharashtra, India, by appointment.

What happens during the first consultation?+

We discuss your goals, health background, food preferences, routine and any challenges. This helps shape your personalised nutrition approach.

Will I receive a personalised meal plan?+

Where appropriate, you will receive practical meal guidance and recommendations based on your needs, preferences and lifestyle.

Do I need to follow a strict diet?+

No. The focus is on realistic, flexible habits rather than unnecessary restriction. Recommendations are individualised.

Can I book follow-up sessions?+

Yes. Follow-ups can help review your experience, address questions and adjust your plan as your needs change.

What should I prepare before my appointment?+

Bring any relevant reports, a list of medications or supplements, and an idea of your usual eating routine. You can also prepare questions in advance.
