A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Good nutrition starts with understanding you.

My aim is to make nutrition feel less overwhelming and more connected to the way you actually live.

HELLO, I'M Harshala Dhavale

Your partner in a more balanced approach.

I'm a registered dietitian based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, supporting individuals and families through practical, personalised nutrition guidance.

I believe that food is about more than nutrients. It is connected to culture, family, enjoyment, routines and the moments that make up everyday life. Nutrition advice should recognise all of that.

My work focuses on helping you understand your choices, build realistic habits and feel supported as your needs evolve. There is no single plan that works for everyone, and your experience should reflect that.
